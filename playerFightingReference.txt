Here is a reference to all the different attacks and hitboxes that the 
player will have, and how they can combo. I am using some of the ideas 
from the google  slide, as well as some of my own ideas:
	
First Off, I am thinking of having 3 seperate fighting styles.
	Fighting Style 1: The Hummingbird
		This is a special style that can be unlocked after beating the
		game and restarting on master mode. It gives a little bit more
		of a challenge to the game. 
			Hitboxes:
				1. Left & Right Player Fist - Humming Fist - Left Click
					* Used for lightning fast punches. Extremely fast, 
					can be combo'd into a sideswing punch.
				2. Right Player Fist - Sideswing Punch - Right Click
					* Used for combos.
				3. Right Leg - Slice Kick - Keyboard_R
					* Used for combos and light damage, stuns for a second
					in a combo.
				4. Left Leg - Double Kick - Keyboard_E
					* Used for heavy knockback and ground stun on non-bosses
				5. Both Legs - Ground Pound - Keyboard_F
					* Used for double jump teching and dodge teching, stuns
					in area of effect.
					
	Fighting Style 2: The Human
		This is the normal fighting style of the game.
				1. Left Player Fist - Light Attack - Left Click 
				2. Right Player Fist - Heavy Attack - Left Click
				3. Right Leg - Slice Kick (Medium Attack) - Keyboard_R
				4. Both Legs - Ground Pound - Keyboard_F
				
	Fighting Style 3: Superhuman Strength
		This is a fighting style unlocked somewhere in the middle of the
		game, potentially it could be locked behind going through the levels
		because of something like "the researchers are trying to figure out
		more about what's going on with you, so they don't want you to take
		any risks using your superhuman strength yet."
			1. Left Player Fist & Right Player Fist - Light Attack - Left Click
			2. Right Player Fist - Grab/Throw (after timer) - Right Click
			3. Both Legs - Ground Pound - Keyboard_F
			4. Heat Action - Beast Unleashed - Keyboard_Q
			5. Both Legs while in air - Piercing Impact Kick (Heavy Mini Heat Attack) - Keyboard_E
